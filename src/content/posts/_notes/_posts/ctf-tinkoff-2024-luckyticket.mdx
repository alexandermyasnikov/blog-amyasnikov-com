### Flag Acquisition Strategy

* Register the user manually, since a captcha must be entered
* Buy N tickets and record the purchase time of each ticket
* Compute the time difference so that the server generates the ticket number at the time we need. To do this:
  * Generate tickets locally in the time range [now - M seconds, now + M seconds]
  * Find the numbers of the purchased tickets and compute the time difference for each ticket
  * Find the average difference, or the median, or simply take the K-th result
* Eat tickets until the luck level reaches 100%
  * If the purchased ticket turns out to be lucky, eat it
