### Reasoning

Known:
- `N` — modulus
- `e1` — first exponent
- `e2` — second exponent
- `c1 = F ^ e1 mod N` — first encrypted message
- `c2 = F ^ e2 mod N` — second encrypted message

Unknown:
- `F` — the flag

Multiply `c1` and `c2` by the powers `p` and `q`:
`(c1 ^ p) * (c2 ^ q) = F ^ (p * e1 + q * e2) mod N`

`F == (c1 ^ p) * (c2 ^ q)` if `p * e1 + q * e2 == 1`

Use the property:
`d ^ s = d ^ (s * (N ^ k)) mod N` for any `d`, `s`, `N`, `k`

That is, `p * e1 + q * e2 = N ^ k`
That is, `gcd(p, q, e1, e2) = 1`

Find `p` and `q` from the equation above, and then `F`:
`F = (c1 ^ p) * (c2 ^ q)`
