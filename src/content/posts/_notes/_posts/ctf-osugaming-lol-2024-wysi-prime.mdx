We need to find two prime numbers `p`, `q` consisting only of the digits `2` and `7`.

### solve.py Script

Obviously, if `p*q == n`, then `(p*q) % k == n % k` for any `k`.
We use a breadth-first search to find the numbers `p` and `q`.
Starting with `p=0` and `q=0`, we grow the numbers by prepending a digit `2` or `7` until we find a pair whose product equals `n`.

Having found `p` and `q`, we compute the secret exponent and decrypt the message.
